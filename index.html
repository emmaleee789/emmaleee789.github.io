Research Experience

Research Intern Dec 2025 – Present

Math Magic · Advisor: Xiaowei Zhou · Project Lead: Heliang Zheng

Co-developing Hi3D 3.0, a 6.6B-parameter image-to-3D foundation model with an MMDiT flow-matching backbone. Designed the two-stage generative space — a TRELLIS-style sparse convolutional autoencoder for structure followed by a vecset VAE for fixed-size latent tokenization — and co-designed the semantic conditioning pipeline (dense DINOv3 via joint attention, pooled SigLIP2 via AdaLN, modality-decoupled 3D RoPE). Built the REPA alignment teacher to supervise denoiser features entirely within the 3D latent space. Diagnosed and corrected a systematic velocity deficit in flow-matching transport using MAFM modulus matching.

Research Assistant May 2026 – Present

Zhejiang University · Advisor: Xiaowei Zhou

Investigating occluded-region failures in image-to-3D flow matching, traced to unsupervised conditional velocity covariance. Proposed a directional second-moment matching loss that supervises the Jacobian-implied covariance against single-sample even-order targets, preserving the flow-matching optimum while restoring branch commitment through sampling dynamics. Preparing submission to ICLR 2027.

Research Assistant Dec 2024 – Jun 2025

Zhejiang University & University of Western Australia · Advisors: Xuequan Lu, Xinkui Zhao

Recast Score Distillation Sampling as a degenerate Schrödinger Bridge and proposed TraCe, a distillation framework that rebuilds the bridge between the current rendering and its text-conditioned denoised target, refreshing both endpoints at every optimization step. Validated on 3D Gaussian Splatting across 83 prompts and 120 views, outperforming SDS, VSD, CSD, SDI, and ISM on CLIP Score, GPTEval3D, and ImageReward. Published at NeurIPS 2025.
