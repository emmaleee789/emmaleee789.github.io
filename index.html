Research

Dec 2025 —

Research Intern, Math Magic

Advisor: Xiaowei Zhou · Project Lead: Heliang Zheng

Co-developing Hi3D 3.0, a 6.6B-parameter image-to-3D foundation model on an MMDiT flow-matching backbone. Designed its two-stage generative space, built the REPA alignment teacher that supervises denoiser features entirely within the 3D latent space, and traced a class of generation failures to transport dynamics rather than model capacity.

May 2026 —

Research Assistant, Zhejiang University

Advisor: Xiaowei Zhou

Identified occluded-region failure in image-to-3D flow matching as blunted anisotropy of the conditional velocity covariance, which the standard objective leaves unsupervised. Proposed a directional second-moment matching loss that restores branch commitment through sampling dynamics while preserving the flow-matching optimum exactly.

Dec 2024 — Jun 2025

Research Assistant, Zhejiang University & UWA

Advisors: Xuequan Lu, Xinkui Zhao

Recast Score Distillation Sampling as a degenerate Schrödinger Bridge and proposed TraCe, which rebuilds the bridge between the two distributions the optimization actually connects. Validated on 3D Gaussian Splatting across 83 prompts and 120 views, outperforming SDS, VSD, CSD, SDI, and ISM.
